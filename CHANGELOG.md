# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (2026-07-25)

- Nudged the keystone line up a touch so it clears the teleport buttons other
  addons add to the dungeon icons.

## 1.0.0 (2026-07-23)

- Initial release: your current keystone shown as `[Dungeon +Level]` under the
  rating in the Group Finder's Mythic+ Dungeons tab.
- Hover for the keystone's own tooltip, shift-click to link it into chat.
- With no key in your bags, points at your vault when a Mythic+ reward is
  waiting, otherwise at the keystone NPC. Silent for characters with no
  Mythic+ score this season.
