# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-07-29)

- Your other characters' keystones now list down the right-hand side of the
  Mythic+ tab, opposite the weekly best block, as class-coloured
  `Name` / `Dungeon +Level` pairs. Text only, no window. Hover a row for that
  keystone's own tooltip, shift-click to link it into chat — same as your own
  line, using the real item link recorded on that character.
- The list holds this week only. It empties at the weekly reset and refills as
  you play each character, so a character you have not logged into since reset
  has no entry rather than a stale one.
- Options panel: toggle the alt list, always show realm names, clear the store.
  Also `/smk alts` and `/smk clear`.

## 1.0.1 (2026-07-25)

- Nudged the keystone line up a touch so it clears the teleport buttons other
  addons add to the dungeon icons.

## 1.0.0 (2026-07-23)

- Initial release: your current keystone shown as `[Dungeon +Level]` under the
  rating in the Group Finder's Mythic+ Dungeons tab.
- Hover for the keystone's own tooltip, shift-click to link it into chat.
- With no key in your bags, points at your vault when a Mythic+ reward is
  waiting, otherwise at the keystone NPC. Silent for characters with no
  Mythic+ score this season.
